# E-05 AI and perception (S-02)

**Goal:** assimilated enemies that perceive by sight (blocked by cover), light and noise, escalate patrol → suspicion → search → hunt, and are stronger than Lukas.

**GDD sources:** S-02, M-09, M-10, M-14, D-23, D-24, D-96; enemy table in `03 - Historia y Mundo/03 - Personajes.md` and `04 - Contenido/03 - Enemigos y NPC.md`.

## Deliverables
`Scripts/AI/EnemyBrain.cs` (state machine), `AlertState.cs`, `Perception.cs` (sight cone + raycast against `Environment`/cover, light ×2 alert speed, noise), `AlertDirector.cs` (highest alert per zone, max 2 active per room, ≤ 6 active AI overall, `AttentionFull` response, transit lockdown D-96), `EnemyDefinition.cs` SO, `Enemies/ED_Alto.asset`, `ED_Rastrero.asset`, `ED_Boca.asset`, `ED_Mudo.asset`, prefabs `Characters/Enemy_<Type>` containing `PH_<Type>`.

## Tasks
- `E05-perception`: pure `PerceptionModel` for the alert accumulation (testable), `Perception` component for queries. Sight never goes through cover (`Cover` markers + colliders). Lit by the flashlight → alert speed ×2.
- `E05-alert-director`: registry of enemies (registered by the director, no singleton: it is a service in the Floor scene registered through `Services`), highest state → `HighestAlertChanged`, hunt events, lockdown of stairs/elevator during a hunt.
- `E05-alto`, `E05-rastrero` (H2), `E05-boca`, `E05-mudo` (H5): NavMeshAgent movement, patrol points from room spawns, attacks/grabs raising `PlayerAttacked`/`PlayerGrabbed`. Only El Alto and El Mudo grab (C-64): a grab intent per attack closes to 1.6 m, plays the `Grab` clip and `GrabPull` holds Lukas at the clip's anchor (Alto 0.86 m, Mudo 0.57 m); a carried Spike fires automatically; no grab across low cover. La Boca and El Rastrero never grab. A hunted enemy that crosses a sealed door breaks the seal in ~30 s (M-14).
- Doors (M-14, C-68): a closed door is a carved wall. In patrol or suspicion an enemy never crosses it. In search or hunt `EnemyBrain.Route()` sends it to the near side of the closed door on the way (picked again every 0.5 s, dropped once the goal is reachable). Within `DoorLeaf.EnemyReach` 1.8 m it stops and faces the door, and `DoorLeaf` opens it after `SystemsBalance.enemyDoorOpenDelaySeconds` 5 s (`DoorEnemyWait`, pure). Back to patrol or suspicion cancels the wait. Lukas opening the door ends it. A sealed door only breaks under a hunting enemy (`sealBreakSeconds` 30 s). `HackPanel` opens a door it unlocks at once.
- Each state change plays the state cue (no on-screen indicator, M-10).
