# Product Overview

## Project

Single-player survival-horror / roguelite videogame currently developed as a playable PC demo / vertical slice.

## Core Fantasy

The player explores a hostile environment while being deliberately weaker than the threats around them.

Survival depends primarily on:

- exploration
- resource management
- situational awareness
- risk evaluation
- avoiding unnecessary combat
- learning from previous runs

## Genre

Primary:
- Survival

Secondary:
- Survival Horror
- Roguelite

## Confirmed Inspirations

### Empty Shell

Reference for:

### Holstin

Reference for:

## Confirmed Core Mechanics

- Six-slot inventory.
- Enemies are generally stronger than the player.
- Procedurally arranged rooms.
- Mandatory key rooms.
- Death restarts the current scenario/run.
- Cloning is the narrative justification for respawning.
- Previous runs may be referenced through a terminal.
- Camera may transition between isometric and over-the-shoulder perspectives.
- Low-poly visual direction.
- Raw / pixelated textures.
- CRT-inspired presentation.

## Design Priorities

1. Tension over power fantasy.
2. Resource scarcity over abundance.
3. Readability over graphical complexity.
4. Meaningful exploration over large environments.
5. Interacting systems over isolated mechanics.

## Scope

The current goal is a polished playable demo, not a complete commercial game.

Avoid designing systems only for hypothetical post-demo content unless the current demo requires them.

## Canon Rule

Do not invent game mechanics, story details, progression systems, enemies, weapons, items, rooms, characters or lore that have not been explicitly approved.

When information is missing, mark it as `TBD` and isolate assumptions from confirmed project decisions.
